Fixes from a code review of sync, accounts and login.

- Sync no longer misses an item edited while it runs, or removes one
  it skipped.
- Switching accounts never shows the previous account's items or
  shares.
- Login follows redirects only within the server address you typed.
- An item that can't load offers Retry instead of spinning.
- An empty collection shared with you to edit can now be added to.
- Fixes to the scanner, the add form and market settings.
