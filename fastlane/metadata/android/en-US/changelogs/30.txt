Reliability and privacy fixes from two audit passes.

- Items shared with you no longer appear in your own
  collection, search or home feed.
- Wiping one category no longer empties the whole app until
  the next sync, and playlists keep the server's order.
- Sync no longer skips an item edited in the same second as
  another, or strands rows behind a partial sweep.
- A photo whose location data cannot be stripped is refused
  rather than uploaded.
- Failed artwork uploads are reported, not silent.
