Star ratings, and items stay linked to the search result you picked.

- Rate items from one to five stars, then sort and group your
  collection by rating. Setting a rating needs Crate 1.15.0 or later
  on your server.
- An item added from search stays linked to the result you picked, so
  enriching it no longer looks it up again and lands on another one.
- The first sync after updating is a full one, to fetch ratings.
